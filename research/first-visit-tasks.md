# First-visit tasks

Three uncoached tasks for real first-time users. They test whether someone can get an answer, explain why to trust it, and name a next step. A developer walkthrough (PR videos, scripted browser checks) is not a substitute.

**Status: no sessions run yet.** Do not fill the results table with guesses or with teammates who have seen the app.

## Who

- Plays in at least one Sleeper dynasty league. Has not used Dynasty Ticker.
- Mix of phone and iPad or desktop. Note the device and the league format (Superflex or 1QB, TE premium or not).
- Five people finds most of the big problems. Stop there and fix before running more.

## Setup

- Open the live site or the preview in a fresh private window. No league loaded, no saved trade.
- Ask them to think out loud. Do not point, hint, or name buttons. If they are stuck for two minutes, note it as a fail, then help and move on.
- Record the screen if they agree. Otherwise take timestamped notes.

## Tasks

Read each task exactly as written.

1. **Find a value.** "You're curious about Brock Bowers. What is he worth right now? Is that more or less than a 2027 first-round pick?"
   - Done when they state the price and compare it to a 2027 first.
   - Follow-up: "Where does that number come from? Would you trust it?"

2. **Check an offer.** "Someone offers you Drake London and a 2027 second for Puka Nacua. Is that a good deal for you?"
   - Done when both sides are built the right way round and they read the verdict.
   - Follow-up: "Why does it say that?" Note whether they mention the package adjustment when one shows.

3. **Identify a team move.** "Connect your own Sleeper league. Find one trade that would make your team better, or decide that you shouldn't trade right now. Tell us why."
   - Done when their league is loaded and they name a trade (or a reason not to trade) using what the app showed.
   - Follow-up: "Why would the other manager say yes?"

## What to record per task

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Completed | Yes, yes with help, or no |
| Time | Start to the answer, in seconds |
| First tap | What they tried first |
| Wrong turns | Each back-out, dead end, or misread label, in order |
| Explained the answer | Their words for why the number or verdict is right |
| Trust | Their words for whether they would act on it |
| Device, format | Phone / iPad / desktop; Superflex or 1QB; TEP or not |

## Reading the results

- A task is a problem when two or more people fail it or take a wrong turn at the same place.
- Write the problem as what they experienced ("could not tell which side was theirs"), not the fix they suggested. Open a ticket with the quote.
- Pageviews and visit counts do not show whether anyone understood an answer or came back. Do not use them as a stand-in.
