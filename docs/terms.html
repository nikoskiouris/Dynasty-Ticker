What this is

Dynasty Ticker is an unofficial, free, open-source viewer and trade helper for public Sleeper dynasty leagues. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Sleeper, the NFL, NFLPA, KeepTradeCut, or any team or player. Team names, logos, and player likenesses belong to their owners.

Your league data

You are responsible for only loading leagues you are allowed to view. The app uses Sleeper’s public API from your browser. If Sleeper rate-limits, changes, or blocks that API, the ticker can fail. We do not control that.

Values and odds

Trade values start from bundled Sleeper dynasty trades across many public leagues, mixed with KeepTradeCut-style rankings. The ticker can also show a separate league board inferred from this room’s completed trades; that overlay stays off until you apply it. Who-would-you-rather votes are public and slightly nudge the board for everyone. Playoff and title odds come from a Monte Carlo simulation. None of that is advice, a valuation service, or a guarantee. Lineups, injuries, and Sleeper scoring can make the board wrong.
