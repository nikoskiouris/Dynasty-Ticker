# Trade prices and projections acceptance criteria

- Rankings, player pages, the calculator, league views and shared trades use the one published 12-team Superflex full-PPR dynasty board.
- Connecting a league keeps the draft and leaves published prices the same.
- A player needs at least three trades from three league seasons in the last 210 days. A second asset present in every recent package with that player makes the price unavailable.
- Unpriced players remain searchable. A trade containing an unpriced piece identifies the missing price and withholds the value comparison.
- The calculator and shared trade show the market value gap and identify any adjustment for fewer, better pieces. League prices are comparisons of market value.
- Sleeper weekly and full-season PPR projections can appear for players without historical stats or a trade price.
- The current rookie pick class remains selectable through August. The generic pick board declines by round and by year.
- Training and scoring exclude deals with missing or unsupported consideration. A frozen earlier fit evaluates the later window. The report includes coverage, shape-specific side gaps and a matched alternative comparison.
- A failed refresh preserves the last valid snapshot. Publishing a new model version stages prices and its history under that model's key so the running release retains access to its own data.
