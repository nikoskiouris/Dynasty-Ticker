# Brag Plan: Dynasty Ticker

## What is this app?
Dynasty Ticker is the live desk for a Sleeper dynasty league: this week's scores, a roster scout, a trade verdict, and the record book.

## The angle
The league is not a spreadsheet. It is a desk with a ticker. You pick a job, and the board answers. Sports-broadcast energy, the product's own words, no startup fog.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark desk. A mint live mark. Then the site's own line, big: "Your league. Pick a job."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The landing jobs arrive one by one after a username is typed: See this week, Scout a team, Make a trade, League history.
- A live scoreboard with two fictional matchups and the real claim: 4,000-season playoff odds.
- The calculator's real verdict language: "Desk verdict / Fair, leans your way," with You send and You receive.

## Outro / punchline
The wordmark lands on a strong beat. "Pick a job." Then dynastyticker.com.

## User flow worth showing
Type a Sleeper username → pick a job → the desk shows live scores, then a trade verdict. Fictional stand-ins only: username `sundaydesk`, teams North / South / East / West, scores and values invented so no real league or manager is on screen. Jahmyr Gibbs is the product's own public example player.
