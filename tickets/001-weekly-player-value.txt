Title
Weekly player value from matchup and usage

Status
Done

Shipped
#57

Leftover
Double-team rate still missing until we have the data.

Priority
P0 — called out as the “magical” feature

Area
League / Teams / new player sheet

Description
Give every relevant player one weekly score built from opponent strength plus that player’s recent usage, not from dynasty rank.

Inputs to blend into one number (and show the pieces, not only the score):
- Opponent strength (pass/run, coverage, recent fantasy points allowed to that position)
- Target share
- Drop percentage
- Double-team rate
- Past games (last 3–6, plus season)

The point is a startable weekly value a manager can trust, not another KTC clone.

Why
“If you can find a way to look at a player, analyze his matchup based on opponent strength and pull his past games — target share, drop percentage, double team rate — into a value, that would be fucking magical.”

Current product
Roster sheets rank by dynasty market value. Scores and awards use Sleeper box scores. There is no NFL matchup or usage model (target share, drops, double teams).

Acceptance
- Open a player and see a weekly score plus the four usage/matchup inputs.
- Same player, easy vs hard opponent: the score moves in the right direction.
- Missing stats fail visibly (“no target-share data”) instead of pretending the score is complete.
- This number is labeled as weekly, never mixed up with dynasty value on the same badge.
