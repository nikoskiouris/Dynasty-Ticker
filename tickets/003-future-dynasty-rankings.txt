Description
Hybrid, not a replacement. Keep the pick. Do not turn a 2027 1st into a fake Jeremiah Smith stock.

For the next rookie class only (one year out, not 2028+), a first shows:
- the pick (2027 1st)
- whose it is (original owner)
- what place that roster is now
- who stored mocks have at that slot, in parentheses

Example: `2027 1st from Niko · 4th (Jeremiah Smith)`.

Store a few first-round mock drafts. Dynasty slot comes from current place (last now = 1.01). College names get no trade value. The number on the row is still the pick’s market price.

Keep current-year drafted names as they are (Cam Ward on a used 2026 1st). Later years stay pick labels.

Why
“Future dynasty rankings — just a year out (instead of saying 2027 1st it says Jeremiah Smith with a value maybe just a ranking rather than a numeric value).”

Current product
Used picks can show the drafted player plus value. Undrafted future picks stay “2027 1st from X” with a pick-market number. No prospect board.

Acceptance
- Next-year 1sts still read as a pick, with owner, current place, and mocked name in parentheses.
- Rank is the manager’s current place, not a made-up 8,400 college value.
- Later years stay pick labels until we deliberately extend this.
- Source of the stored mocks is named on the pick vault.
