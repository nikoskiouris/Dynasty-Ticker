Description
Two complaints, one fix:

1. Do not land on the calculator. Landing should not open it. After a league is loaded, calculator is a quick link, not the Trades homepage.
2. The first Trades screen has to explain itself: partners, find deals, log, then calculator. A blank two-sided board with no orientation is the current first impression.

Recommended default Trades room: Partners (match) or a one-screen “here is your window + two next trades,” with Calculator in the room strip and as a small control on Start / Call.

Landing “Make a trade” should not `data-room="calculator"`. Point it at Partners or that overview.

Why
“Don’t have the calculator upon opening but have a quick link on the main page.”
“This trade page I have no idea what’s going on upon initially entering it.”

Current product
Landing is not the calculator. “Make a trade” still opens `trades` / `calculator`. `DEFAULT_ROOMS.trades` is `calculator`. Trades tab subtitle is “Calculator, partners, deals.” Calculator shell is a two-team builder with no first-run story. Partners and Find deals already exist as other rooms.

Acceptance
- Fresh “Make a trade” / Trades tab does not open the calculator.
- First Trades view states what to do in one sentence (find a partner, or shop a name).
- Calculator is one tap from that view and from the league Start board.
- Old `?view=calculator` links still work.
