Title
Trade match invents positional needs

Status
Done

Shipped
#77

Priority
P0 — concrete bug from the note

Area
Trades → Partners (`docs/modules/trade-match.js`, power position percentiles)

Description
Needs are “your position room’s dynasty-value percentile vs the rest of the league,” not “can this roster start a real player there.”

So a team with Isaiah Likely and Dalton Kincaid can still be labeled NEEDS TE if McBride/Bowers rooms sit higher on the board. Then every generated match is a tight end deal. That is the bug they hit.

Fix the need grade so two startable players at a position (especially TE in a 1-TE league) are not a need. Use starter slots, startable quality, and maybe “would this player start on most teams,” not only percentile of KTC-ish value. Do not let one mild gap dominate every match.

Why
“It also said my friend NEEDS a TE and all of his trade matches were for tight ends, he has Isaiah Likely and Dalton Kincaid, so just super wrong.”

Current product
`gradePositionNeed` treats percentile ≤ 0.42 as a need and ≤ 0.28 as critical. Position value is the top-N dynasty values at that position. Matches then fill `takePositions` from those needs.

Acceptance
- A roster with two viable TEs (Likely + Kincaid class) is not “NEEDS TE.”
- Matches for that roster are not all TE-for-TE.
- A roster with no startable TE still shows a TE need.
- Add a regression test with two mid-tier TEs vs a league that has one elite TE room.
