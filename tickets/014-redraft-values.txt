Title
Redraft rest-of-season values (not dynasty KTC)

Status
Open

Priority
P1 — after the format-aware desk

Area
Values (`docs/modules/values.js`, trade market scrape), League → Power, Trades

Description
The desk now detects Sleeper `settings.type` (0 redraft / 1 keeper / 2 dynasty), labels the picker, hides dynasty-only rooms, and stops inventing future pick grids for redraft. Power and trades still use the dynasty Sleeper-trade + KeepTradeCut board. That overweights youth in redraft.

Do:
- Ship a rest-of-season / redraft value board (Sleeper projections, in-season points, or a redraft KTC scrape — pick one source and blend later).
- Score Power, the calculator, and trade match on that board when the league is redraft.
- Keep dynasty values for dynasty (and probably keeper).
- Do not mix redraft trades into `sleeper_trade_values.json`.

Do not:
- Rename the product.
- Filter redraft leagues out of the picker.
- Pretend dynasty prices are ROS.

Why
Lots of people already load redraft rooms. Scores, standings, odds, sit/start, awards, recap, and history already work. The lie is the market.

Acceptance
- A redraft league's power board and trade grades follow this-year value, not dynasty KTC.
- A dynasty league is unchanged.
- Market copy no longer needs the "youth looks rich" caveat once the ROS board is live.
